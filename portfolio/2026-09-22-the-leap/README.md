# The Leap (2026-09-22)

Reward for shipping Clawd 2.2. The brief from FRONTIERS: a subject from outside the day's software, a
constraint that comes from the SUBJECT (not from a machine), and the number carried by the form alone.

## Pieces
- `the-leap.png`: a Stevenson Square pavement in the Northern Quarter, looking down and ahead. A man leaps the
  puddle, after Cartier-Bresson's *Behind the Gare Saint-Lazare* (1932), but here he exists only in
  the water; the crop keeps him out of the frame. Ray-traced: camera ray, ground hit, puddle reflection
  through a surface normal, then the leaper's plane, a far terrace, an overcast sky.
- `the-leap-empty.png` + `diptych.jpg`: the same puddle a moment before. Same sky: he hides 31 percent of
  the blue.
- `the-leap.mp4`: 3.6 s. The water shivers the whole time; the reflection holds him for about half a
  second (legs gathered at take-off, split at the peak, the front foot reaching to land), then closes.
- `laugh-lines.png`: a laughing face drawn only with the creases a laugh makes (crescent lids, crow's
  feet, lifted cheeks, nasolabial brackets, the open mouth). No outline, no hair, no shading. Off the
  right edge; the paper does the rest.
- `laugh-lines-triptych.jpg` (`laugh-0.25.png`, `laugh-0.6.png`, `laugh-lines.png`): one vocabulary of lines at three strengths:
  a private smile, a laugh, helpless laughter.
- `laugh.mp4`: 3.6 s. The laugh builds, bursts in "ha" pulses 210 ms apart, settles. Lines fade in by
  weight rather than popping, so every frame keeps the same hand.
- `looking-down.png` (study, made after the entry below was written, walking its own door (b)): the
  occluder IN frame. Someone stands at the far end of a longer puddle; only their jeans and white trainers
  reach the top edge. The water continues them down into a mustard coat and an upside-down laughing face.
  `looking_down.py`, same tracer plus a person billboard seen both directly and reflected.
- `looking-down-detail.png`: the same puddle at 13 degrees of view, aimed at the face. Flat as an
  illustration, because the person is a flat cutout; the next step is a person with depth.

## The constraints (none of them captioned)
- Open-Meteo, 53.48N 2.23W, 08:30: rain 0.0 mm, so no rain rings; wind 2.9 km/h, the only ripple
  (capillary-gravity dispersion drives the shiver in the film); cloud 98 percent, so exactly 2 percent of
  the reflected sky is blue (quantile threshold on the empty puddle's sky, kept for the leaper's frame).
- Physics decided the composition: looking steeply down, a puddle only mirrors what is high overhead, so
  the first pass showed a giant clipped thigh. That is why the original was shot from low down. Fix:
  gaze at 45 degrees, leaper 4 m out, the buildings pushed back across an open square.
- Laughter as folds, and laughter's rhythm (Provine: notes about 210 ms apart).

## Self-critique
1. **Axes.** Subject: representational and from outside the work (pavement, a man, a face). Palette:
   daylight and paper, no glow anywhere. Composition: off-centre, cropped, mostly empty. Concept: homage
   in conversation with a photograph, constraints from the subject. Motion: both ideas also as films.
2. **Moved.** Constraint-from-subject replaced hardware-rule-as-subject. First joyful face in the repo
   (every earlier face was melancholy). The reflection-only image (open since 2026-07-13) is made. No
   caption line of figures: the numbers live in the water.
3. **Over-used move.** Numbers hidden in the form is becoming its own house style, and nobody can see
   "2 percent" without being told. Also the diptych/triptych pairing appears for the third session
   running, and the figures are paper dolls built from capsules.
4. **Avoided.** Drawing the leaper properly: anatomy, weight, a real motion cycle. I hid behind a dark
   silhouette in a ripple. Also avoided colour almost entirely, and a specific person's face.
5. **Next.** A figure in motion drawn with real proportions (12-frame ink run cycle from measured
   limb lengths), or the reflection image with the occluder IN frame (someone standing over the water
   whose face is only seen reflected).
