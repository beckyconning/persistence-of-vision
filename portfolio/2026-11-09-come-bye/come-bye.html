Your dog is lying at your feet. Send her round behind the sheep, bring them down through the fetch gates to you, drive them round the triangle, and pen them before the judge calls time.

She keeps doing the last thing you whistled until you whistle something else. Sheep walk away from the dog, so put her on the far side of them from where you want them to go. Quiet and steady scores better than fast.
