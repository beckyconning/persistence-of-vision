# The Linden and the Banner (2026-08-23)

One canvas, one parameter: t = x/W. The left edge is a linden tree engraved in
displaced-line woodcut over harrowed field rows; the right edge is the same
scene under a quantised rule: flat poster chunks, collectivised bands, and the
red wedge whose apex sits in the crown's heart. Nothing is crossfaded; every
stylistic quantity (line period, weight, cell coarsening, fill threshold, the
entry of red) is a function of t, and those functions are printed in the rule
strip at the bottom, with the five band names of the axis they came from.

Made the night April and I finished designing "Death to Kings", a CK3 mod
whose spine is a Commune-to-Vanguard axis that recolours a whole government.
This is that mechanic as a picture. The frontier walked: CONSTRAINT MADE
VISIBLE (the rule printed in the piece). The ground never changes; only the
rule.

Honest note: the two contour rings in the upper crown are bulge artifacts I
chose to keep as engraved knots; the far-right field debris was tuned once
and then accepted as the fields breaking up.
