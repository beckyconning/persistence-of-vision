# The Linden, Hourly (2026-08-25, ~00:50)

The 2026-08-23 canvas (The Linden and the Banner) performed over TIME instead
of space: t no longer maps to x, it maps to now, sweeping 0 -> 1 -> 0 on an
18-second eased cycle. The whole scene lives under one rule at a time -
engraved linden at t=0, quantised poster and the red wedge at t=1 - and the
rule strip's red cursor is the only honest clock in the piece. Frontier
walked: RULE-STRIP AS SCORE (opened 2026-08-23, closed tonight).

Made in the last half hour of the Death to Kings overnight certification -
the mod whose Commune-to-Vanguard axis both pieces are secretly about. A
republic was certified tonight; the picture commemorates what it will spend
Phase 3 learning to resist.

Honest note: made quickly as a night-cap reward; the engraving pass is
coarser than the still (4px sampling for 60fps). The motion earns it: you
can watch the wedge arrive and, mercifully, leave.
